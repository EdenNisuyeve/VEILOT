01 / The problem

More connected devices. More blind spots.

Devices ship fast and stay in service for years, often without a security review. Each one is a possible way in.

  • Weaknesses ship with the product

    Default passwords, outdated components and unsigned firmware are built in at the factory.

  • Invisible to standard tools

    Most IoT devices can't run security agents, and many never make it into the asset inventory.

  • A way into everything else

    One exposed camera, monitor or charger can open a path to the systems behind it.

03 / How it works

From unknown device to resolved risk.

Build a live inventory

Passive network analysis identifies every connected device, including the ones nobody registered.

Inspect each device in depth

Firmware, settings and traffic are checked for known vulnerabilities, hidden passwords and unusual behavior.

Rank by real exposure

Each finding is scored by how exploitable it is and what the device can reach, so teams fix what matters first.

Contain, then fix

Isolate exposed devices through your network controls, then follow guided steps to patch and verify.

04 / What we protect

Built for the devices you actually run.

Every type of device fails in its own way. VEILOT checks for the weak points specific to each one.

01Medical devices

Patient monitors, infusion pumps and wearable sensors

  • Unauthenticated device commands
  • Patient data exposed in transit
  • Legacy software that can't be patched

02Connected vehicles

Fleet telematics, EV chargers and in-vehicle systems

  • Location and driver data leaks

05 / The platform

Capabilities for devices that can't protect themselves.

  • Agentless discovery

    Identifies and classifies devices from network traffic, without installing software on them.

  • Firmware analysis

    Inspects binaries for known vulnerabilities, hard-coded secrets and insecure components - before devices ship.

  • Behavioral baselines

    Learns how each device normally communicates and flags deviations as soon as they appear.

  • Context-aware scoring

    Weighs every finding by exploitability, network reach and the device's operational role.

  • Full lifecycle coverage

    One view of risk from development builds to devices operating in the field.

  • Fits your security stack

    Sends findings to SIEM, SOAR, ticketing and network access control tools your teams already use.
